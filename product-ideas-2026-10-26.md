### The candidate that cleared the bar and still died — Xero BAS/GST lodgement snapshot

Best demand of the run: verbatim, audit-grade, across **five organisations**. *"No tracking or record of
an amendment in Xero"*; after lodging, GST balances *"no longer agree to the BAS that was lodged"* with
users needing *"full transaction support for the BAS lodged"*; the NZ **Publish** function **removed**
from the new GST report at **188 votes**, parked **Not in Pipeline**.

**Three independent kills, any one sufficient:** (1) **XBert's own help article states Xero does not
expose the lodged statement through its API** — so the product cannot read the lodged figures and would
have to reimplement AU BAS label logic well enough to survive an **ATO audit**; (2) **XBert** is a
dedicated, priced, Australia-only incumbent at **A$8.40–A$118.80 per client/month** plus
**A$34.80/user/month**; (3) **QuickBooks Online ships the feature natively** — mark-as-lodged,
prior-period adjustments, and an audit report of affected transactions. Xero also **already has Published
Reports**, so the snapshot half is native and the workaround costs **$0**.

## Risks and watch-outs

- **The structural read, at fifteen runs.** The method works — it produces reliable, well-evidenced
  *negative* findings fast, and this week correctly killed a bar-clearing candidate in one session.
  **What is exhausted is the search space, not the method: vendor-feature-board gaps in SMB SaaS are
  systematically occupied, because the same boards that reveal a gap also recruit the founders who fill
  it.** If next week also returns zero new rows, the recommendation is to **change the search space, not
  the query shapes** — candidate pivots are recorded in the log.
- **A free incumbent is a harder kill than a cheap one.** IDs 001, 006 and 024 are now all anchored at or
  near $0 by someone else. None is a problem a better product solves.
- **Method corrections worth carrying.** `discuss.bubble.io` **does not exist** — the real host is
  `forum.bubble.io`, and four runs deferred a typo. **Verify a hostname resolves before calling a channel
  dead.** A working forum also **does not vouch for its network**: `contractortalk.com` works while
  `electriciantalk.com`, `plumbingzone.com` and `painttalk.com` all returned zero.
- **`capterra.com` is usable under Mode B, but query products, not phrases** — it returns verbatim, dated,
  star-rated review text when approached by product name, and nothing when approached by complaint wording.
- **Channel caution.** `indiehackers.com` is a channel of **sellers, not buyers** — use it for competitor
  discovery and pricing, never for buyer complaints. `forum.bubble.io` is demand-only: **Zeroqode alone
  publishes ~871 plugins**, which rules Bubble out as a build target.
- **No income or profit claim is made anywhere above.** Every price, vote count and quotation traces to a
  cited link seen in search result text this run; the Acquire.com fee structure and the Flippa asking
  prices are explicitly flagged as conflicting or aspirational and are not used as valuation evidence.
